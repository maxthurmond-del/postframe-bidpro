# PostFrame BidPro — Continuing Project Brief

This is the master project for an installable post-frame construction bidding and takeoff application.

## Working rule
Preserve existing estimating rules unless the user explicitly changes one. Update the PWA package and in-chat version together whenever available.

## Current product goals
- Installable PWA for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Windows
- Works offline after first load
- Saved bids and reusable templates
- Automatic post-frame material takeoffs
- Internal costing, optional markup, tax calculation, and customer proposal
- Building preview driven by dimensions, materials, colors, and openings

## Important framing rules already established
- Gable-end and sidewall dimensions are outside face of post to outside face of post.
- Interior bays are centerline of post to centerline of post.
- Posts use 4 ft embedment plus 2 ft above clearance.
- Purlins are Doug Fir 2x6, spaced every 2 ft up the roof, and 2 ft longer than the bay.
- Interior post lines use paired trusses; gable ends use one outside truss.
- Post-hole concrete is 0.5 cubic yard per hole.
- Taylor Metal trim is ordered in 12 ft pieces.
- Snap-Loc roofing requires CDX roof sheathing.
- Metal Board & Batten siding requires CDX wall sheathing.
- Proposal price is after optional markup and states “plus tax”; tax is not included in the displayed proposal amount.
